Patterns, with a little chaos.

This is where my creative coding work lives. Every piece starts with a rule that repeats, like petals around a flower or cells in a honeycomb, and then I let random() bend the rule a little so nothing comes out the same twice.

The picture on this page is my Homework 7, remade in p5.js. The daisy and the honeycomb are both built from for loops. New in this version: a bee that glides around using noise(). Click the picture to re-roll everything, and hover over the hexagons to light them up.
